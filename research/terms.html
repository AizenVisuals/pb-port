A free fan game

PB Ranked is a free, non-commercial fan game made by Sondre Norheim. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Game Freak or Creatures. Pokémon and the names of its characters are trademarks of their owners. There are no ads, purchases or donations.

Playing fair

  • Play your own account. No cheating, no exploiting bugs on purpose, no losing on purpose to help someone climb.
  • Keep trainer names and tournament names friendly: nothing hateful, sexual, threatening or impersonating someone.
  • Found a bug? Use "Report a problem" in the game.

We may change a name, reset a rating, remove a result or close an account that breaks these rules.

The game can change

PB Ranked is in development. Pokémon, moves, balance and rules change as the game grows, ratings may be reset at the start of a season, and the game may be paused or shut down. It is provided as it is, without guarantees.

Content and takedown requests
